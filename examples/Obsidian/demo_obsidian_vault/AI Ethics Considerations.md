# AI Ethics Considerations

Critical ethical considerations in AI development and deployment.

## Core Principles

### Fairness and Bias
- **Algorithmic Bias**: Systematic discrimination in AI systems
- **Data Bias**: Biased training data leading to unfair outcomes
- **Mitigation Strategies**: Bias detection, diverse datasets, fairness metrics

### Transparency and Explainability
- **Black Box Problem**: Understanding AI decision-making
- **Interpretable AI**: Making AI systems explainable
- **Stakeholder Communication**: Clear explanation to non-experts

### Privacy and Data Protection
- **Data Rights**: User control over personal information
- **Differential Privacy**: Protecting individual privacy in datasets
- **GDPR Compliance**: Legal requirements for data handling

### Accountability and Responsibility
- **Human Oversight**: Maintaining human control
- **Liability Questions**: Who is responsible for AI decisions?
- **Audit Trails**: Tracking AI system behavior

## Current Challenges

1. **Large Language Models**

2. **Autonomous Systems**
   - Decision-making in critical situations
   - Safety guarantees and testing
   - Human-AI collaboration

3. **Surveillance and Control**
   - Facial recognition ethics
   - Predictive policing concerns
   - Social credit systems
